# 🛍️ Vinted Product Analytics Case Study

> An independent Product Analytics case study exploring buyer behavior,
> conversion, and potential friction points in the Vinted marketplace.

## 🎯 Business Question

### How could Vinted improve the buyer journey and increase purchase conversion?

I am exploring:

- 🔎 Where potential drop-offs occur
- 👤 How users behave throughout the journey
- 📊 Which metrics can help measure conversion
- 💡 Which product improvements could be tested
